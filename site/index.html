    Method, official data sources & project history

    Tides come live from NOAA CO-OPS (9414290), rain from NWS gridpoint MTR 85,105, street bowls and drains from USGS 3DEP 1m/5m bare-earth lidar, and visual verification from USGS HIVIS (1,443 nationwide river cameras) and Caltrans CWWP2 Districts 1–12. View the archived Oct 25 build-day demo.
